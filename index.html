How it works

Simple as 1 – 2 – 3

From browsing to your doorstep (or pickup), ShareFIT makes renting effortless.

1

Find your fit

Browse thousands of curated pieces by size, style, brand, or occasion — filtered to your city.

2

Book & confirm

Pick your rental dates, send a request, and get approved by the lender — usually within hours.

3

Wear it, return it

Receive the piece, rock it, then ship or drop it back. We handle the damage deposit securely.

4

Earn as a lender

List items you love but rarely wear. Earn 80% of each rental, paid directly to your account.
